    - Oat Milk
    - Kale Chips
    - Lingonberry Juice
    - Meatballs and Poteitr
    - Baked Poteitr
    - Smoked Fish
  - Increased the duration of the following foods from 20 to 25 minutes:

    - Cooked Seal Blubber

* Better Creature Drops
  - Increased Memorial Coal drop chance from Krigen (both variants) from 20% to 30%.
  - Increased Frostfire and Thunderblood Essence drop chances from Fallen Warriors from 50% to 75% each.

  - Increased level-up chances for Ashlands Charred Warriors, Marksmen, Twitchers and Asksvin to 15% after defeating Fader.
  - Enabled one-star Voltures with a 10% level-up chance after defeating Fader.
  - Deep North creature difficulty remains unchanged.
