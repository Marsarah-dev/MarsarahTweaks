C. New Features
------------------------------
* Paving Without Leveling 
  - Adds an extra option to the Hoe allowing stone paving without altering terrain height or smoothing.
  - Automatically disabled when the following mods are detected:
    1. Advanced Terrain Modifiers
    2. ValheimTerrainTools
    3. MyDirtyHoe
    4. Paved Road No Level
    5. Pathside Assistance
    6. Shack Better Paths

* Adjustable Hoe & Cultivator Radius
  - Added radius adjustment on mouse-wheel for Level Ground, Pathen, Paved Road, Paving, Cultivate, and Grass.
  - Mouse-wheel radius adjustment is in 1 m increments, with a 1–7 m range (Pathen has a minimum of 2 m, since it does nothing at 1 m).
  - Disabled camera zoom while adjusting the radius of the above terrain operations.
  - Added individual radius memory for all six operations, retained when switching tools or selections during a game session.
  - Automatically disabled when the following mods are detected:
    1. Hoe Radius (aedenthorn)
    2. TerraHoe
    3. Treadwell
    4. Advanced Terrain Modifiers
    5. ValheimTerraformPlus
    6. SmarterHoe
    7. ValAdvancedHoe
    8. ValheimTerrainTools
    9. MyDirtyHoe      

* Added Standardized Food Durations
  - Increases food durations based on preparation type and biome progression:
    - Raw edible ingredients: 15 minutes.
    - Simply cooked meats: 25 minutes.
    - Prepared meals: 30 minutes.
    - Only increases durations where needed, leaving other foods unchanged.

* Black Forest Root Drops
  - Added Wood, Core Wood, and Resin drops to wild roots in the alternate Black Forest biome, respecting Progression Halt.

* Crown of Roots Changes
  - Reduced Crown of Roots Coin cost from 3k to 2k, and made it repairable at a lvl 3 Workbench.
