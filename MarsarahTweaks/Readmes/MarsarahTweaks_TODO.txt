C. New Features
------------------------------
* Better Wood Yields
  - Added a new feature under Grind Reduction that changes wood drop distribution to 30% regular Wood and 70% specialty wood (Core Wood, Fine Wood, Ashwood and Timberwood) for Pine, Birch, Oak, Ashlands and Deep North trees.
  - Changes apply only to half-logs, leaving standing trees, full logs, stumps and Yggdrasil Shoots unchanged.
  - Total wood quantities remain unchanged.
  - Respects Automatic Progression Halt.

* Improved Frostfire Damage
  - Added a new feature under Balance to improve Deep North Frostfire weapon damage across all 12 weapon variants.
  - Changed base elemental damage from 88 Frost / 12 Fire (10 Fire for Dagger) to **80 Frost / 40 Fire** at quality 1.
  - Preserved the existing physical damage and elemental upgrade scaling (+3 Frost / +3 Fire per quality level).

* Improved Dyrnwyn
  - Added a new feature under Balance to improve Dyrnwyn's damage.
  - Increased base Fire damage from 10 to 20 and added +5 Fire damage per upgrade level (previously 0).
  - Preserved the original Slash damage and upgrade scaling.

* Potion Upgrade
  - Added a new feature under Grind Reduction to convert surplus lower-tier recovery meads into stronger versions at the Cauldron.
  - Added three new recipes using a 3:1 conversion ratio:
    - 3 Minor Healing Meads → 1 Medium Healing Mead (Cauldron level 3).
    - 3 Minor Stamina Meads → 1 Medium Stamina Mead (Cauldron level 4).
    - 3 Medium Healing Meads → 1 Major Healing Mead (Cauldron level 5).
  - Conversions are instantaneous and require no additional ingredients or fermentation.
